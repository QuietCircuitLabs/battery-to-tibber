Keep the battery level of your manually added Tibber vehicle up to date from a selected battery-reporting device in Homey or a Flow. Send the actual percentage, or optionally adjust it for your car's AC charge limit so Tibber can plan charging towards that limit. Tibber keeps control of charging. Vehicle and home-charger connection checks can be combined without GPS; a home-location check is optional. Tested with Kia EV9 and Zaptec; other integrations need validation with their users. This independent community app uses Tibber's unofficial app API.

Full Homey access is needed to find and read your selected source devices and, if you opt in, request vehicle status through the Kia app. Location permission supports the optional home check and address selection. Optional saved Tibber credentials stay in the app's internal storage on Homey; the developer does not receive them. Setup includes a safe same-value write test before automation is enabled.
